Data files used by the Python lessons on learn.lotfinejad.ir (loaded into the browser workbench; no upload, no network from learner code).

day.csv, hour.csv
  Bike Sharing Dataset, UCI Machine Learning Repository.
  Fanaee-T, Hadi, and Gama, Joao. "Event labeling combining ensemble detectors and background knowledge." Progress in Artificial Intelligence (2013).

i94_traffic.csv
  Metro Interstate Traffic Volume, UCI Machine Learning Repository (hourly I-94 westbound traffic near Minneapolis-St Paul, with weather).
  Donor: John Hogue. Licence: CC BY 4.0. https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/492/metro+interstate+traffic+volume
